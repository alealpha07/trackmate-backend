Who is responsible

TrackMate is a free, non-commercial project. The controller for the personal data processed in the app and on this website is:

Alessandro Prati
Email: alealphatv@gmail.com

What data is processed

  • Account: your username, your password (stored only as a hash, never readable), your bio, your profile picture, your level and experience, the vehicles you choose and the date you accepted the terms. TrackMate doesn't ask for your email, name or phone number.
  • Tracks and travels: the routes you plan or save, and the GPS positions recorded while you record or navigate a track. Your location is used only while you record or navigate, or when you center the map on yourself, and only after you allow it.
  • Posts and friends: the posts you publish with their photos, likes, saved posts, friends and friend requests, and your quests.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you logged in, your language, and the server's access logs (IP address, date and time, requested page, status code and browser user agent).

The app and the web page also keep your theme, language, map style and recently loaded map tiles on your device. They never leave it.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To run TrackMate for you (account, route planning, recording, navigation, posts, friends and quests): performance of the contract under the terms of use, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • To keep the service secure (access logs, limits on login attempts and requests): our legitimate interest in a secure, working service, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • To remove content reported as illegal: our legitimate interest and legal obligations, Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR.

There is no advertising, no analytics, no profiling and no sale of data. The only cookie is the session cookie, which is needed to log in.

Who can see your data

  • Your username, profile (bio, picture, level, number of friends) and posts, with their track, photos and likes, are visible to every TrackMate user.
  • A post link you share (/p/…) opens for anyone who has it, without an account. Search engines are asked not to index it.
  • Travels you record on a posted track, yours or another user's, are visible to every TrackMate user in that track's leaderboard and travel comparison: your username, the date and time, duration, distance, speeds and the recorded GPS trace, from where you started recording. They stay visible as long as the track is posted. If you don't want a travel to be visible, you can delete it.
  • Tracks you haven't posted and the travels on them, saved posts and quests are visible only to you.

Hosting and other services

  • Hosting: the server runs in a data center in Germany operated by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Access logs are deleted after 14 days at most, unless they are needed to investigate a specific security incident.
  • Map tiles: the app and the web page load the map images straight from Stadia Maps, Inc. (USA). Your device sends them its IP address and the map area you are looking at. Stadia keeps its server logs for about 14 days, and transfers outside the EU rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See the Stadia Maps privacy policy.
  • Search, routes and elevation: when you search for a place or plan a route, the TrackMate server, not your device, sends the search text, the coordinates or the map area to Stadia Maps and Photon by komoot GmbH (place search), the Overpass API run by NextGIS OÜ (Estonia) or FOSSGIS e.V. (Germany) (map data), Valhalla on the FOSSGIS e.V. server (turn-by-turn directions) and the Copernicus elevation data on Amazon Web Services. These requests contain neither your IP address nor your username.
  • Location on Android: the app reads your position through the location service of Google Play services on your phone. Google processes that under its own privacy policy.
  • Fonts and the map library are served by the TrackMate server, so your browser doesn't contact Google Fonts or other content networks.

How long data is kept

  • Your account, tracks, travels and posts: until you delete them or your account.
  • The session: until you log out, or 30 days after you last used TrackMate.
  • Access logs: 14 days at most.
  • Emails you send: as long as needed to answer them.

Your rights

  • Download your data: in the app, Settings › Download my data gives you a ZIP file with everything stored about you.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings › Delete account removes your account and all your data at once.
  • You can change your profile, posts and tracks in the app at any time.
  • You also have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 18, 20 and 21 GDPR). Write to the email address above.
  • You can complain to the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali or to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live.

Minimum age

TrackMate is for people aged 16 or older.
